A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious testing.
Clear communication.

I’m a Software Quality Assurance Engineer with experience testing web applications, mobile apps, SaaS platforms, and AI-powered products. My work includes designing test cases, validating end-to-end workflows, exploring edge cases, documenting defects, and retesting fixes.

I focus on understanding how people actually use a product—not just whether a happy path works. I aim to make findings useful to developers and product teams through clear steps, expected and actual results, and actionable context.
